## Day 32 — Think Like a Marketing Strategist: Grow This Brand

### What I Built
A single-file HTML app (React via CDN + Babel JSX) that teaches marketing
strategy reasoning rather than generating content directly — explaining
"what is this?" and "why does it matter?" before every decision, across
Business, Personal Brand, or Random Client mode.

### Flow
1. Welcome screen introducing marketing strategy
2. Mode selection: Use My Own Business / Build My Personal Brand / Random Client
3. Audience understanding (product = expertise + story in Personal Brand mode)
4. Platform selection with per-platform reasoning
5. Content pillar selection — exactly 3 of several offered
6. 30-day roadmap with weekly goals (Week 1 = POV + bio/profile in Personal Brand mode)
7. Randomized marketing event (viral post, podcast invite, copycat, follower spike) with consequence
8. Growth Report: Audience Understanding, Platform Strategy, Content
   Strategy, Growth Potential, Best Decision, Biggest Mistake, 3 Marketing Lessons
9. "How to ask Claude" reusable prompt card after every major section

### My Playthrough
| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Mode | Personal Brand |
| Platforms | LinkedIn (primary), X/Twitter (secondary), newsletter (owned audience) |
| Content Pillars | Thought Leadership, Personal Story, Audience Education |
| Best Decision | Including Personal Story rather than two "safe" educational pillars |
| Biggest Mistake (flagged by the app) | Nearly skipping Week 1 bio/profile optimization to post content early |

### What I Learned
Niche clarity turned out to be an audience decision made before any content
gets planned, not a content decision made afterward. Every later choice in
the flow — platforms, pillars, Week 1 roadmap goals — traced back to how
clearly the audience was defined at the start. The app's insistence on
optimizing bio/profile before publishing (in Personal Brand mode) reframed
"audience-first" from a marketing slogan into an actual sequencing rule.
