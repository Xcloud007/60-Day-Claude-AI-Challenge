## Day 11 — ATS Resume Optimizer

### What I Did
Used Claude as an ATS Expert to analyze a resume against a 
Business Development Executive job description and generate 
a fully optimized, recruiter-ready resume.

### ATS Match Score
- Before Optimization: 61/100
- After Optimization: 85+/100 (estimated)

### Key Gaps Identified
- Missing keywords: Lead Generation, Pipeline Management,
  Revenue Growth, Client Relationship Management
- Missing skills: CRM tools, B2B Sales, Sales Forecasting
- Weak sections: Professional Summary, Achievements

### What Changed After Optimization
- Added JD keywords naturally throughout the resume
- Rewrote professional summary with role-specific language
- Restructured skills section with ATS-friendly keywords
- Strengthened experience bullet points with action verbs

### Key Learnings
- ATS filters resumes before any human sees them
- Keyword matching is the most critical factor for ATS score
- Same experience described differently can double ATS score
- Claude can act as a full recruitment panel in one prompt
