Concept · Headline Framing

Headlines are written to make you click — not always to tell the truth

Publishers earn money and attention through clicks. Writers often use vague danger, big numbers, or emotional loaded words to turn an ordinary story into something urgent-sounding.

Why it matters: A misleading headline can shape your opinion before you even read the article — and most people never read past the headline at all.

Concept · Emotional Manipulation

Content that makes you FEEL something spreads faster than content that informs

Fear, outrage, awe, and tribal pride all trigger shares and comments. Social platforms reward engagement — so posts are often engineered to hit an emotional nerve rather than deliver balanced facts.

Why it matters: When a post makes a decision for you emotionally before your logical brain catches up, you're more likely to share misinformation or form a snap judgment about people or events.
