# Interview Talking Points

## Most Impressive Achievements to Mention
- Managed multiple real offline clients independently as a student
- Completed graphics designing course and delivered client projects simultaneously with academics
- Building AI-powered applications through 60-Day Claude Challenge with zero coding background

## Key Stories to Prepare

**"Tell me about a time you managed a client"**
→ Talk about your freelance design clients, how you understood their brief, proposed solutions, and delivered results

**"Why BDE despite a CSE background?"**
→ Tech + communication combination is rare; AI tools knowledge will help in market research and data tracking

**"How do you handle rejection?"**
→ Talk about iterating on client feedback during freelance projects

## Areas Recruiters Will Likely Ask About
- Why Business Development and not a tech role?
- Do you have CRM experience? (Be honest — say you're learning Zoho CRM)
- How do you generate leads? (Talk about outreach, research, networking)
- Where do you see yourself in 2 years?
