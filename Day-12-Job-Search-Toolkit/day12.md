## Day 12 — Job Search & Personal Branding Toolkit

### What I Generated
A complete 12-section job search toolkit using Claude including:
- ATS Cover Letter
- Recruiter and Hiring Manager outreach emails
- LinkedIn connection request
- Referral request and follow-up email
- 30-second professional introduction
- Top 10 suited job titles
- Skill gap analysis with learning roadmap
- Personal brand summary and positioning statement
- Interview talking points

### Target Role
Business Development Executive — Lucknow (Hybrid)

### Key Learnings
- Claude can act as a Career Coach, Recruiter, and 
  Branding Consultant simultaneously
- Personal branding is about positioning your existing 
  experience — not inventing new ones
- A fresher with real client experience can compete 
  strongly with the right framing
- Skill gap analysis + learning roadmap turns weaknesses 
  into an action plan
