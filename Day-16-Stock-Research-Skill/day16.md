## Day 16 — Stock Fundamental Research Custom Skill

### What I Did
Created a reusable Stock Research Custom Skill inside Claude 
Projects. Once configured, the skill automatically generates 
a complete fundamental research report just by typing a 
company name — no prompt repetition needed.

### Skill Configuration
- Platform: Claude Projects (Custom Instructions)
- Effort Level: Low
- Skill Type: Reusable Financial Research Workflow
- Coverage: Indian and Global listed companies

### What the Skill Generated Automatically
Each report included:
- Company overview and exchange listing details
- 5-year revenue and profit trend charts
- Key financial metrics (ROE, ROCE, P/E, EV/EBITDA)
- Valuation assessment (overvalued / fairly valued / undervalued)
- Shareholding pattern with visual breakdown
- Bull case vs Bear case analysis
- Risk rating and overall outlook
- Analyst price targets

### Most Surprising Insight
Claude generated visually rich, chart-embedded HTML reports 
with real financial data for each company — just from typing 
the stock name. The skill remembered the full research 
framework without needing the prompt again.

### Key Learnings
- Claude Projects allow saving reusable instruction sets 
  called Custom Skills
- Once a skill is configured, only the input (stock name) 
  is needed — the workflow runs automatically
- The same skill works equally well for Indian and global stocks
- AI can replace hours of manual research with a single input
- Reusable workflows are the real productivity unlock in AI
