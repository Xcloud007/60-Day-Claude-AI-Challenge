## Day 4 — Chain-of-Thought Prompting

### What I Learned
Chain-of-Thought prompting makes Claude reason step by step instead of 
jumping to an answer. This produces more structured, personalized, 
and accurate outputs.

### Comparison
- Normal prompt: gave a generic roadmap with no personalization
- CoT prompt: gave a detailed, month-by-month personalized roadmap 
  based on my specific background and goals

### Key Takeaway
For complex tasks like planning, analysis, or problem-solving — always 
use step-by-step prompting. The output quality is dramatically better.
