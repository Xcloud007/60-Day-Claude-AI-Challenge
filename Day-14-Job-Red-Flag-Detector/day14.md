## Day 14 — Job Red Flag Detector

### What I Did
Used Claude as an AI Job Red Flag Detector to analyze two 
sample job descriptions and company information — one 
technical and one non-technical role.

### Jobs Analyzed
1. Junior Full Stack Developer (Tech Startup)
2. Marketing Executive (Marketing Agency)

### Risk Scores
| Role | Risk Score | Verdict |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Junior Full Stack Developer | 87/100 | ❌ Avoid |
| Marketing Executive | 78/100 | ❌ Avoid |

### Biggest Red Flags Found

**JD 1 — Full Stack Developer:**
- "Junior" role demanding 5+ years experience
- 24/7 support requirement
- 8-month-old startup with no funding disclosure
- Hidden relocation inside a "remote" role

**JD 2 — Marketing Executive:**
- One person expected to do 8+ different job roles
- "Available whenever required" = no work-life balance
- High employee turnover reported in online reviews
- No salary disclosed despite "competitive" claim

### Most Surprising Insight
Both jobs were marketed positively but scored 75+ on risk.
The language used ("rockstar", "work hard play hard", 
"wear multiple hats") are deliberate signals of toxic 
workplace culture hidden inside exciting-sounding JDs.

### Smart Interview Questions Generated
1. What does a typical working day look like for this role?
2. What is the on-call schedule and how is it compensated?
3. What is the salary range and review structure?
4. What is the average tenure of team members in this role?
5. Is the remote policy permanent or subject to change?

### Key Learnings
- Job descriptions use coded language to hide red flags
- "Junior" + excessive requirements = exploitation signal
- Always research company reviews before applying
- No salary disclosure = negotiation disadvantage for candidate
- Claude can protect job seekers from bad opportunities
