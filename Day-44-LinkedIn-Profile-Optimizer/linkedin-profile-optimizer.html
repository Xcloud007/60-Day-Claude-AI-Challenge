7-Day LinkedIn Activation Plan

Profile edits are step one — this is what makes it work
Day 1

Make all profile changes

  • Swap headline to Option 2 or 3 from the Rebuild
  • Replace About section with the new Hook + Story + Proof + CTA
  • Rewrite the 3 experience bullets with action verbs
  • Add all 10 recommended skills, pin the top 3
  • Add a profile photo check: clear face, good lighting, no group photo crop
Day 2

Publish Post #1 — "I just rebuilt my LinkedIn"

I just rebuilt my LinkedIn from scratch. Here's what I got wrong for months: → My headline said 4 job titles and nothing about what I actually do → My About section opened with "I'm passionate about technology" (so does everyone else's) → I had 4 skills listed. LinkedIn lets you list 50. None of this is really about LinkedIn. It's about being specific instead of generic — on your profile, in your work, everywhere. Currently building real AI/ML projects as a B.Tech student, documenting the process here. What's one thing on your profile you've been putting off fixing?
Day 3

Send 10 strategic connection requests

🎯Target: final-year/recent-grad AI-ML peers, 2-3 people actively posting about AI projects, 2-3 people in Digital Marketing/BDE roles (your job-search adjacent field), 2 professionals at companies in Lucknow/Noida/Greater Noida.
Hi [Name] — came across your profile while exploring the AI/ML space. I'm a B.Tech student building real-world AI projects and would love to connect and follow your work.
Day 4

Comment on 5 posts in your target space

🧩Value Comment formula: Agree (one line showing you understood the point) + Add Insight (a specific detail, example, or counterpoint from your own experience) + Ask a Question (invites the author or others to reply, boosting your visibility in the thread).
Day 5

Publish Post #2 — lessons-learned / hot take

Unpopular opinion: my "non-technical" internship taught me more about working with people than any coding project has so far. I spent 3 months writing content and designing campaign posters at InAmigos Foundation. No Python, no models — just deadlines, feedback, and communicating clearly. Now that I'm deep in AI/ML, I still lean on that experience constantly: explaining a model isn't that different from explaining a campaign. Technical skills get you in the room. Communication is what keeps you there.
Day 6

Engage + expand

✅Reply to every comment on both posts within a few hours. Send 5 more connection requests using the Day 3 template, targeting people who engaged with your posts.
Day 7

Review the numbers

📊Profile views: a jump after Day 1-2 confirms the headline/About rewrite is working — search and curiosity clicks are up.
📊Connection acceptance rate: above ~40-50% on cold requests is healthy for a student profile; lower means tighten your targeting or personalize the note further.
📊Post engagement: comments matter more than likes for reach — if Day 2/5 posts got replies, double down on that voice; if not, adjust the hook line next time.
