## Day 24 — Business Strategy & Investment Readiness Report

### Startup
AI Career Copilot for Indian Freshers
Prepared by: AI Co-Founder & Growth Advisory Desk
Date: 26 July 2026

### Startup Summary
- AI-powered career copilot combining resume ATS scoring 
  and role-based skill-gap checking in one product
- Targets final-year students and freshers aged 18-24 
  in Tier 2/3 Indian cities
- Core problem: ATS rejection, skill misdirection, 
  unaffordable coaching, and 4-5 fragmented tools
- MVP: AI resume upload + ATS score + fixes + skill-gap checker
- Pricing: Free (₹0) → Starter (₹99/mo) → Pro (₹299/mo)
- Validation: Founder-built prototype, organic LinkedIn 
  traction, zero paying users yet
- Prior scores: Problem Severity 78/100, MVP Readiness 
  70/100, Customer Clarity 58/100, PMF Potential 52/100

### Business Reality Check

**Who pays?**
The fresher directly (B2C today). No institutional payer 
yet — colleges and placement cells are a future B2B2C path.

**Why do they pay?**
Only if the tool visibly increases their odds of getting 
shortlisted. Willingness to pay is tied to a felt outcome 
(a callback), not the feature itself.

**How will they discover the product?**
Currently through the founder's personal LinkedIn presence 
and 60-Day Challenge audience. No repeatable, scalable 
discovery channel exists yet.

**Biggest Growth Risks:**
- Distribution is one person's LinkedIn audience — not a channel
- Incumbents (Internshala, Unstop, Naukri) own the traffic 
  and can bundle AI features quickly
- Solo-founder bandwidth caps parallel channel testing

**Biggest Monetization Risks:**
- Job search is a bounded 2-4 month need — recurring 
  monthly subscription may be the wrong pricing shape
- Free substitute (DIY ChatGPT/Claude) already exists
- ₹99/month is a real decision for Tier 2/3 users, 
  not an impulse purchase

**Weakest Assumptions:**
| Assumption | Risk | How to Test |
|------------|------|------------|
| Monthly pricing fits the need | Job search is bounded-time; structural churn after placement | Test one-time Placement Pass vs monthly plan |
| Organic content drives conversion | LinkedIn traction is engagement, not purchase intent | Track free-to-paid conversion from real traffic |
| Resume + skill-gap is enough value | Users may want job listing/application step too | Ask non-converters directly what stopped them |

### Pricing Recommendation
| Option | Structure | Fit |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| Monthly only | ₹99/₹299 per month | Fights bounded-need pattern |
| One-time Pass | ₹399 flat for 90 days | Matches how students think about spend |
| Hybrid (recommended) | Pass as primary + monthly as addon | Captures both user types |
