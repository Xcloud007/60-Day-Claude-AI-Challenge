# Rupesh's Weekly Productivity Plan

*Assumption: ~1–2 hrs on weekdays, 3–4 hrs on weekend days. Adjust blocks up/down based on your actual free time.*

---

## Monday — Data Science Foundations
- **1 hr:** Python or SQL practice (pick up where you left off in your roadmap — pandas/numpy or query drills)
- **30 min:** Review last week's 60-Day Challenge task, plan today's/this week's module

## Tuesday — Claude Challenge Deep Work
- **1–1.5 hr:** Work on current 60-Day Challenge task (prompt engineering technique, custom skill, or Football Intelligence Hub module)
- **20 min:** Organize outputs into your per-stock/per-task HTML folders, ready for GitHub upload

## Wednesday — Stats & ML
- **1 hr:** Statistics or ML concept study (whichever your roadmap has queued — e.g. scikit-learn, XGBoost basics)
- **30 min:** One LeetCode or StrataScratch problem

## Thursday — Stock Research + Claude Skills
- **1–1.5 hr:** Apply your custom Claude skill for stock fundamental research on one company (Indian or global)
- **20 min:** Log findings into your stock folder structure

## Friday — Wrap-Up & GitHub Sync
- **45 min:** Finish/polish the week's Claude Challenge deliverables
- **30 min:** Upload week's work to GitHub (Xcloud007/60-Day-Claude-AI-Challenge) via web drag-and-drop
- **Optional 15 min:** Quick football reading/highlights as a wind-down

## Saturday — Deep Work Block
- **2–3 hr:** Bigger data science project time — portfolio piece, Kaggle dataset, or bigger Challenge module (like Football Intelligence Hub build-out)
- **30 min:** Football — catch a match or review weekend fixtures (guilt-free, it's your interest too)

## Sunday — Review & Plan Ahead
- **1 hr:** Review the week — what got done, what slipped, adjust the roadmap
- **1 hr:** Light stock market reading/portfolio check-in
- **30 min:** Set next week's priorities (which Challenge task, which DS topic)
