## Day 5 — Context Engineering

### What I Learned
Context Engineering means giving Claude relevant background 
information before asking your question. This transforms generic 
AI responses into personalized, actionable outputs.

### Comparison
- Without context: Claude gave a generic productivity plan 
  for anyone
- With context: Claude gave a plan tailored specifically to 
  my schedule, goals, challenges, and the 60-Day Challenge

### Key Difference
Prompt Engineering = HOW you ask
Context Engineering = WHAT background you provide before asking

Both together = significantly better AI outputs

### Key Takeaway
Before asking Claude anything complex, always dump your relevant 
context first. Think of it as briefing Claude like a personal 
assistant who needs to know your situation before helping.
