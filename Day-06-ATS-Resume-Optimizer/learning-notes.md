## Day 6 — ATS Resume Optimization

### What is ATS?
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) automatically filters resumes 
before humans see them. Most companies use it to shortlist candidates.

### What I Did
Used Claude as an ATS Expert, Resume Writer, Recruiter, and 
Hiring Manager simultaneously to optimize my resume.

### Key Learnings
- Keywords matter more than design for ATS
- Clean single-column formatting beats fancy layouts
- Action verbs + measurable results make experience stand out
- A professional summary at the top grabs recruiter attention fast
