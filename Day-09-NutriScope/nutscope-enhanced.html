Educational Disclaimer

NutriScope is an educational tool, not medical advice.

The calculations here (BMR/TDEE, macro and micronutrient targets, risk flags) use general population formulas and reference values. They do not account for medical conditions, medications, lab results, pregnancy, or individual metabolic differences.

Nutrient values for foods are typical estimates and can vary by brand, preparation method, and region. This app does not diagnose, treat, or replace guidance from a registered dietitian, physician, or qualified healthcare provider. If you have a health condition or are making significant dietary changes, please consult a professional.

Nutrition Data Sources

Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017)National Institute of Nutrition, ICMR — primary reference for regional food nutrient values.
USDA FoodData CentralUsed for cross-referencing macro/micronutrient estimates of common ingredients.
ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2020)Reference for RDA-style targets (protein, iron, calcium, vitamins).
Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (1990)Used for BMR estimation, widely validated for healthy adults.
WHO/FAO Micronutrient RequirementsReference ranges for vitamin and mineral adequacy used in risk analysis.

All values in this app are approximations for educational purposes and should not be treated as laboratory-grade data.

NutriScope runs entirely in your browser. No data leaves your device.
