### Most Valuable Data Assets at Risk
- Location and mobility patterns
- Financial transactions and spending habits
- Communication patterns and social graph
- Entertainment preferences and screen time
- Shopping behavior and purchase history

### Top Privacy Recommendations from Dashboard
1. Review app permissions — especially location access
2. Use a privacy-focused browser for daily searches
3. Enable two-factor authentication on all accounts
4. Audit which apps have access to Google account
5. Limit cross-app data sharing where possible

### Key Learnings
- 15 apps across 6 companies creates an extensive 
  digital footprint most people never think about
- Google ecosystem concentration is the single biggest 
  privacy risk for most Indian users
- Facts vs Estimates labeling is crucial — AI should 
  never claim certainty about inferred personal data
- A single dashboard can make abstract privacy risks 
  instantly visible and actionable
- Claude can act as a privacy analyst, data scientist, 
  and cybersecurity consultant simultaneously
