## Day 23 — Customer & MVP Blueprint

### Startup Idea
AI Career Copilot for Indian Freshers — an AI-powered 
platform combining resume optimization, skill gap analysis, 
mock interviews, and job application tracking in one place.

| Metric | Score | Meaning |

| Customer Clarity | 58/100 | ICP defined but needs market validation |
| Problem Severity | 78/100 | Pain is real and urgent |

### Final Verdict
🟡 PROMISING BUT UNVALIDATED
Problem severity and MVP readiness are strong. Customer 
clarity and PMF potential are held back by zero market-level 
validation so far. The 30-day plan exists to move this 
from yellow to green.

### Ideal Customer Profile
| Attribute | Profile |
|-----------|---------|
| Life Stage | Final-year undergrad or graduate within 0-12 months |
| Age / Location | 18-24, Tier 2/3 Indian cities (Lucknow, Kanpur, Indore) |
| Income | Middle/lower-middle — cannot afford ₹15K-50K coaching |
| Digital Behavior | Smartphone-first, active on Instagram/YouTube/LinkedIn |
| Core Need | Affordable step-by-step path to "job ready" |

### Buyer Persona — "Ambitious Ananya"
- Age: 21, final-year BCA, Tier 2 city
- Goal: Land ₹3.5-6 LPA job within 3 months of graduating
- Frustration: Resumes rejected silently, unsure which skills 
  recruiters want, can't afford coaching
- Trigger to buy: Sees a peer's before/after resume win on social media
- Willingness to pay: ₹99-499/month if call-back rate visibly improves

### Top 10 Customer Pain Points
1. Resume gets rejected with no explanation why
2. Doesn't know which skills employers actually want
3. Can't afford ₹15K+ coaching or placement bootcamps
4. Applies to dozens of jobs with no strategy, gets no replies
5. Freezes in interviews from lack of real practice or feedback
6. Unsure if using ChatGPT/Claude correctly for career help
7. No single place to track application progress
8. Generic online advice doesn't match their specific target role
9. Doesn't know how to translate coursework into resume bullets
10. Feels anxious and behind compared to Tier 1 college peers

### Customer Journey
| Stage | Experience | Action |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Awareness | Sees LinkedIn/Instagram post about AI resume scoring | Publish proof-driven before/after content |
| Consideration | Tries a free resume/ATS check | Zero-friction free tool delivering value in under 5 minutes |
| Purchase | Wants deeper help — mock interviews, tailored applications | Low-ticket paid tier after free "aha" moment |
| Retention | Uses platform weekly while job-hunting | Track outcomes, send nudges, show visible progress |

### MVP Recommendation
**Build First:**
- AI resume upload + instant ATS compatibility score with 
  specific actionable fixes
- Role-based skill-gap check (compares resume to target JD)

**Do NOT Build Yet:**
- Full job board — competitors already own this at scale
- Paid courses or certification content — high effort, not validated
- Native mobile app — web-first sufficient to test demand

**Success Metrics (First 30 Days):**
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Free tool completions | 100 users |
| Free-to-paid conversion | 5-10% |
| Resume re-upload / return rate | ≥30% |
| Testimonials collected | 5+ |

### MoSCoW Prioritization
| Priority | Items |
|----------|-------|
| Must Have | AI resume upload + ATS score, actionable fixes, skill-gap check |
| Should Have | Save multiple resume versions, mock interview Q&A, progress dashboard |
| Could Have | LinkedIn profile review, curated free resources, referral incentives |
| Won't Have | Full job board, paid courses, native mobile app, employer tools |

### Pricing Hypothesis
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | ₹0 | 1 resume ATS score + top 3 fixes |
| Starter | ₹99/month | Unlimited scoring, skill-gap check, tailored suggestions |
| Pro | ₹299/month | Starter + AI mock interviews + application tracking |

### Top 5 Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Incumbents copy AI-copilot feature quickly | Move fast on narrow wedge; build trust and content moat |
| Tier 2/3 price sensitivity limits paid conversion | Freemium model; prove ROI before any upsell |
| Solo founder — limited GTM bandwidth | Focus on one channel only (LinkedIn/campus groups) |
| AI/API cost scaling faster than revenue | Track unit economics per user from day one |
| Low initial trust ("another job platform") | Lead every touchpoint with free, provable value |

### 30-Day MVP Plan
| Week | Focus | Key Actions |
|------|-------|------------|
| Week 1 | Narrow the wedge | Build resume upload + ATS scorer; run 10 customer interviews |
| Week 2 | Ship the MVP | Launch landing page + working scorer, no login friction |
| Week 3 | Drive first users | Share in college groups, LinkedIn, communities; collect feedback |
| Week 4 | Test willingness to pay | Introduce ₹99-199 paid feature; track conversion and retention |

### Biggest Customer Insight
The real pain isn't the resume itself — it's the silence 
after applying. Freshers don't know WHY they're being 
rejected. That single insight defines what the MVP must 
solve first — not job listings, not courses, but 
explainable feedback on why applications fail.

### Key Learnings
- Customer clarity requires real interviews — AI analysis 
  alone scores 58/100 until validated with actual users
- Problem Severity of 78/100 confirms the pain is real 
  and urgent enough to build for
- MoSCoW prioritization prevents over-building — 
  knowing what NOT to build is equally important
- ₹99-299 pricing is the sweet spot for Tier 2/3 India 
  where trust must be earned before payment
- The 30-day plan is the most actionable output — 
  it converts a report into daily executable steps
